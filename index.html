Official Governance Document Effective: August 2026

Privacy Policy

Your privacy and faith journey data protection are foundational to Home.cell. This policy details how we collect, safeguard, and respect your personal information.

1 Our Commitment to Member Privacy

Home.cell ("we", "our", or "the Platform") provides digital fellowship infrastructure for church members, cell groups, and Christian believers. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of your personal data and spiritual records.

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or spiritual activity to third-party advertisers.

2 Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Display name, email address, password hashes managed via Google Firebase Authentication, and profile avatar settings.
  • Fellowship Profile Data: Assigned cell group ID, personal testimony, spiritual goals, role badge, and optional location coordinates for cell discovery.
  • Spiritual Activity & Progress: Daily devotional check-ins, prayer petitions, scripture bookmarks, trivia quiz scores, streak counts, and Champions Hub rewards.
  • Communication & Audio Logs: Fellowship Lounge messages, intercession requests, and direct support chat histories with leadership.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Web Push notification tokens, browser device type, and encrypted Firestore session logs for real-time synchronization.

3 How Your Information Is Used

Your data is processed strictly to enable home cell features and enhance community fellowship:

  • Coordinating cell group attendance, leader check-ins, and parish calendar RSVPs.
  • Delivering daily scripture notifications and prayer desk intercession alerts.
  • Computing streak milestones, Champions Hub leaderboards, and referral badges.
  • Enabling direct spiritual support chats with church leadership and cell leaders.

4 Security & Infrastructure

All database operations are secured using Google Cloud Firestore Security Rules, enforcing authentication verification and strict role-based access control (RBAC). Data transmission uses end-to-end TLS/SSL encryption.

5 Your Data Control & Rights

You maintain complete ownership of your profile. You may update your display information, clear your testimony, or request account deletion at any time by contacting our leadership team or visiting Profile Settings.
