<strong>THE HOME.CELL EXPERIENCE</strong>

<br><br>

Home.cell is built around a simple idea:

<strong>Fellowship should not end when the gathering ends.</strong>

Your Home Fellowship can meet physically while Home.cell keeps everyone connected digitally throughout the week.

From conversations and community posts to quizzes, events, resources, testimonies and fellowship activities, everything is designed around meaningful Christian community.

<br><br>

<strong>FIND YOUR HOME FELLOWSHIP</strong>

<br><br>

Discover Home Fellowships available on the platform and find a community that fits you.

Explore fellowship information such as:

<ul>
<li>Fellowship name and motto</li>
<li>Location and address</li>
<li>Gathering schedule</li>
<li>Contact information</li>
<li>Fellowship leader</li>
<li>Member count</li>
<li>Description</li>
<li>Additional fellowship information</li>
</ul>

You can join an existing fellowship or create your own.

<br><br>

<strong>ONE ACCOUNT. MULTIPLE FELLOWSHIPS.</strong>

<br><br>

Home.cell allows one user to belong to multiple fellowships.

You can have different roles in different fellowships while using the same account.

For example:

<br>

<pre>
Fellowship A → Cell Leader
Fellowship B → Member
Fellowship C → Moderator
</pre>

<br>

Switch between your fellowships and immediately enter the environment that matters to you.

<br><br>

<strong>GLOBAL COMMUNITY</strong>

<br><br>

Home.cell connects believers beyond individual fellowships.

The global experience includes:

<ul>
<li>Global Feed</li>
<li>Global Chat</li>
<li>Global Hall of Fame</li>
</ul>

Share encouragement, experiences, thoughts, images and videos with the wider Home.cell community.

Every contribution can retain its fellowship identity, helping people know the community behind the person sharing it.

<br><br>

<li>Quizzes</li>
<li>Events</li>
<li>Activities</li>
<li>Resources</li>
<li>Testimonies</li>
<li>Fellowship content</li>
</ul>

Private fellowship information stays within the appropriate fellowship.

<br><br>

<strong>QUIZZES & COMMUNITY CHALLENGES</strong>

<br><br>

Make learning interactive.

Fellowship members can create and participate in quizzes connected to their fellowship.

The quiz experience supports:

<ul>
<li>Multiple-choice questions</li>
<li>Scoring</li>
<li>Results</li>
<li>Leaderboards</li>
<li>Quiz history</li>
<li>Fellowship-based permissions</li>
</ul>

Members can create quizzes for fellowships they belong to, while fellowship leaders and Super Admins have appropriate moderation controls.

<br><br>

<strong>GLOBAL HALL OF FAME</strong>

<br><br>

Celebrate people making an impact across the Home.cell community.

The Global Hall of Fame brings community achievements together while preserving fellowship identity.

It is a place to recognize participation, contribution and excellence across the platform.

<br><br>

<strong>SHARE PHOTOS & VIDEOS</strong>

<br><br>

Home.cell supports real media uploads directly from the application.

Users can upload:

<ul>
<li>Profile pictures</li>
<li>Feed images</li>
<li>Videos</li>
<li>Fellowship images</li>
<li>Event media</li>
<li>Quiz images</li>
</ul>

Media is handled through Cloudinary while Firebase manages application data, authentication and media metadata.

Users never need to leave Home.cell to upload or view their media.

<strong>SECURITY & PRIVACY</strong>

<br><br>

Home.cell separates global community features from private fellowship environments.

Firebase Authentication protects user accounts.

Firestore manages application data.

Fellowship memberships and roles determine access to private fellowship functionality.

Administrative permissions are enforced through the application's authorization architecture rather than relying only on frontend visibility.

<strong>MEDIA ARCHITECTURE</strong>

<br><br>

When a user uploads media:

<br>

<pre>
User selects media
       ↓
Home.cell
       ↓
Cloudinary
       ↓
Media URL returned
       ↓
Firestore stores URL + metadata
       ↓
Home.cell displays the media
</pre>

<br>

Cloudinary handles the actual media storage and delivery.

Firebase handles the application data.

The entire process happens behind the Home.cell interface.
